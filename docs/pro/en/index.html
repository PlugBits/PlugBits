Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free trial?
Yes. Enter your email address in the "Pro License" tab of the extension's settings screen, and your 14-day Pro trial starts right away. No credit card registration is required.
Does the data I edit pass through an external server?
No. All processing happens inside your browser, and data is saved directly to kintone. The contents of your records are never sent to any external server, including PlugBits servers.
How do I receive my license key?
After your purchase is complete, a license key (in UUID format) is sent to the email address you registered. Enter it in the Chrome extension icon → Settings → Pro License tab.
What if I lose my key?
Enter the email address you used when purchasing and submit it, and we'll send the key to that address again.

Can I cancel?
Because it's a one-time purchase, there's nothing to cancel. Once you've paid, no further charges occur. You can view your receipt using the "Receipt" button in the "Pro License" tab of the extension's settings screen.
What's the difference from the free version?
The free version includes the side panel (shortcuts, history, watchlist, and Spreadsheet View for viewing) and Quick New (add new records). Pro adds editing, saving, and adding rows with Grid Edit, plus copy & paste with Excel.
Do you support corporate purchases or invoice payment?
Currently we only support personal credit card payments. For corporate use, please contact us at support@plugbits.app.
Can I use it with multiple accounts or on multiple devices?
Each license key is intended for use in one browser profile. If you need additional licenses, please contact us.
