Command Palette / Free

Drive kintone with only the keyboard.

Ctrl+/, from anywhere

Switch views, jump to another app, or create a record, all by typing a name and hitting enter.
Fewer trips back to the mouse means navigating kintone just feels faster.

Quick New / Free

You decide which fields to fill in.

Shift+Alt+N for instant entry

kintone forms are built for everyone, so they tend to grow more fields over time. But at the moment you're entering data, you only need a handful of them.
Quick New is an entry modal that shows only the fields you need, in the order you choose. No hunting around, just fill in from top to bottom and you're done in the shortest time possible.
You never leave the list view, and your scroll position stays put after saving.

Spreadsheet View / Free

Open a quick list view whenever you need it

Opens fast as a secondary view

Spreadsheet View isn't meant to replace kintone. It's a secondary view you can open quickly whenever you need it.
You stay close to your original screen while reviewing everything you need in the list.

Side Panel

Your most-used actions, all in one place

Shortcuts

Pin your most-used apps as icons for instant access.

Watchlist

Shows a count for a chosen view, like items awaiting approval, so you can see what's left to do right now.

Record Pin

Pin records you're keeping an eye on to the side panel, and open them instantly whenever you need them.
