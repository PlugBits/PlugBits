How it works

  1. It only changes how the list is displayed. It doesn't touch your records or view settings.
  2. No business data is sent externally. License verification only sends your kintone domain name and key.
  3. It works only through the official API and events, without touching kintone's internal structure.

Installation

  1. Upload the plug-in zip file (download the zip) to kintone's plug-in management
  2. In the target app's settings, add the uploaded plug-in to the app
  3. Open the plug-in's settings screen, enter your key in the "License" field, and save (not needed for the second and later apps in the same domain; if you haven't purchased yet, you can start the trial from "Try free for 14 days")

If you've lost your key: enter the email address used at purchase and submit, and we'll send the key to that address again.

Only 2 settings to configure

To filter by date range, all you need is a single date or date/time field.

  1. Views to show in the tabs, and their order
  2. The date or date/time field to use for the date range

(Optional) Totals to display above the list: choose a number or calculated field.
