How it works

  1. Data is still stored in the subtable as before. Even if you remove the plug-in, the photos and notes stay in kintone.
  2. Your business data is never sent externally. License verification only sends the kintone domain name and key.
  3. It works entirely through official APIs and events, without touching kintone's internal structure.

How to install

  1. Upload the plug-in zip file (download the zip) to kintone's plug-in management.
  2. In the target app's settings, add the uploaded plug-in to the app.
  3. Open the plug-in's settings screen, enter your key in the "License" field, and save. (Not needed for the second and later apps on the same domain. If you haven't purchased it yet, you can start a trial from "Try it free for 14 days.")

If you've lost your key: enter the email address used at purchase and submit, and we'll send the key to that address again.

Only 4 settings to configure

On your form, prepare a subtable that includes an Attachment field and a multi-line text field, plus one space field.

  1. Choose the subtable (table) that will hold the photos
  2. Choose the Attachment field within the table
  3. Choose the note field within the table
  4. Choose the space to display the upload area and gallery
